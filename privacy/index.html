Slot Twist privacy

Steelyard Ventures LLC · Updated October 31, 2026

We collect nothing

Slot Twist does not collect, store or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not use the internet to play.

Your game stays on your device

Your current run, records, unlocks and settings are saved only on your device. They may be included in your own Apple device backups, depending on your settings. Deleting the app removes them from the device.

Permissions

The app does not ask for your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or health data.

Purchases

Slot Twist is paid for once through the App Store. Apple handles the payment and we never receive your card details. There are no in-app purchases.

Pre-release testing

If you test a pre-release version through Apple's testing service, Apple shares crash logs and usage data with the developer, along with any feedback you choose to send. We use these only to fix the game.

Contact

For a privacy question, email help@steelyardventures.com with "Slot Twist" in the subject. If you email us, we receive the address and message you send and use them only to reply.
