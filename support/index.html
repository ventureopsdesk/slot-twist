How to play

Tap SPIN. Each spin costs your bet. Match 3 or more on a lit line to win coins. Tap LINES to play more lines and BET to raise your stake. Wins fill the Vault; fill it to pick a Boost. Reach Vault 12 and crack the Grand Vault to win the run. If you can no longer cover the minimum bet you are busted, and one tap starts a new run. Settings has What Pays, Trophies and How it works.

Coins

Coins are for play only. They cannot be bought, have no cash value, and cannot be won as money, cashed out or transferred. There are no real-money prizes.

Refunds

Apple handles App Store payments. To request a refund, go to reportaproblem.apple.com.

For adults

Slot Twist is for adults 18 and over. It does not offer real-money gambling. If gambling is a problem for you or someone you know, free help is available in the US at 1-800-GAMBLER.
